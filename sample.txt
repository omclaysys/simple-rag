Python is a high-level, interpreted programming language known for its readability and versatility.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines retrieval from a knowledge base with generation from a large language model.

Embeddings turn text into dense vectors that capture semantic meaning, allowing similarity search.

ChromaDB is a lightweight vector database that runs locally and makes it easy to store and query embeddings without any infrastructure.

Chunking text into smaller pieces improves retrieval quality because embeddings work best on focused passages rather than entire long documents.
